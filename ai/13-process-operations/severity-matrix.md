# Severity Matrix

| Severity | Meaning | Typical Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SEV-1` | Full outage, data loss risk, or critical business stop | Immediate incident command and potential rollback |
| `SEV-2` | Major degradation affecting important flows | Fast coordinated response and mitigation |
| `SEV-3` | Partial degradation with workaround | Prioritized response during support window |
| `SEV-4` | Minor issue or low-risk operational anomaly | Track and resolve through normal workflow |

## Notes

- Severity measures impact, not engineering difficulty.
- Priority may still change based on timing, customer commitments, or repeated recurrence.
