# Monitoring Rules

## What To Monitor

- availability
- latency
- error rate
- queue or job health
- deployment health signals
- critical business flow signals where available

## Rules

- Every critical user flow should have at least one health signal or smoke check.
- Alerts should be actionable and tied to an owner.
- Avoid noisy alerts that do not require human action.
- Monitoring should distinguish transient anomalies from sustained incidents where possible.
